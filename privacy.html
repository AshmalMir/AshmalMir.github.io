Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This app is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own edited video clips to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What data is used

  • An OAuth access token for the owner's own TikTok account (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload), used only to upload videos.
  • The video files and captions the owner creates.

How it is stored

Tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to any third party, except TikTok and YouTube when uploading.

Other users

The app does not collect data from any other person. It does not track viewers and has no analytics or advertising.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's app settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services) and delete the local token file.
